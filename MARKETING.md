# Review QR: marketing, sales pitches and ideas

## Positioning in one line

**A good-looking Google review QR code sign, ready to print in two minutes, for $5.99. No sign-up, no subscription, never expires.**

Who it's for: owners and managers of small NZ businesses where customers visit in person (cafés,
restaurants, bars, salons, shops, tradies, motels, clinics, gyms, tour operators).

What they're comparing us with:
- **Doing nothing** or a hand-written "Review us on Google!" sign (no direct link, so few reviews).
- **Free QR generators**: ugly plain codes, and many are dynamic codes that stop working when a trial ends.
- **Review-management subscriptions**: monthly fees and far more than a café needs.
- **Paying a designer**: more money, and a few days' wait.

Our edge: cheap, instant, designed, static (never expires), NZ-made, TV slide included, and it links
into myQR Digital Signage.

## Launch plan

1. Set it live in the admin and buy one yourself (use a real café you know, with permission).
2. Photograph the sign in real places: a café counter, a restaurant table, a salon mirror, a TV. Swap
   these photos into the home page and ads.
3. Offer it to every existing myQR Digital Signage venue (email: "your TVs can collect reviews too").
4. Walk-in pitch 20 local cafés with a printed sample. Leave a card with the web address.
5. Run a small Google Ads test ($10–$20/day) on the keywords above for two weeks. Watch Admin → Traffic.
